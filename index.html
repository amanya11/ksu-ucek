About UCEK KSU Wing

Breaking the long 26 years of only one unit/party in UCEK's history, we have emerged to bring you the KSU wing of our college—standing up for the students, for every need of the student.

We support and aim for Solidarity by uniting every student voice in mutual support, and Social Justice by fiercely standing up against campus inequality and protecting student rights.
