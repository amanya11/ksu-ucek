Kalavedhi — Embrace Culture

Kalavedhi is our cultural wing dedicated to organizing events where students can actively participate in sports, music, arts, and games. It helps students discover and nurture their true talents, enabling them to reach new heights. Through these events, students realize their absolute potential. UCEK has just emerged with its KSU wing and will remain active year-round to organize engaging cultural experiences for all UCEK-ians.
