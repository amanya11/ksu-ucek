Kalavedhi — Embrace Culture

Kalavedhi is our vibrant cultural wing that organizes events for students to actively participate in sports, music, arts, and games, helping them discover and nurture their true talents, enabling them to reach new heights. These events help students realize their potential and understand what they are truly meant for. UCEK has just emerged with its KSU wing and will be active in upcoming years to organize and bring up exciting events for all UCEK-ians.
